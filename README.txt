DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Horizon Furniture is a state-of-the-art, high-converting Shopify 2.0 theme
crafted specifically for modern Furniture, Decor, and Architectural brands.
Built with clean Scandinavian & Japandi aesthetics, it features dynamic 
room lookbooks, room set bundle builders, before/after timber sliders, 
and white-glove delivery shipping calculators out of the box.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 100% Redesigned Japandi & Scandinavian Aesthetic (Warm Cream & Sand Palette)
* Shoppable Room Lookbook Hotspots (Dynamic Pinning)
* Category Bento Grid Showcase Layout
* Room Set Bundle Builder with Instant Batch Add-to-Cart
* Before / After Timber & Staging Comparison Slider
* Sticky Ajax Cart Drawer with Free White-Glove Delivery Progress Bar
* PDP Dimensions & Weight Specification Sheet (Shopify Metafields)
* White-Glove Bulky Furniture Shipping Notice
* Product Care & Assembly PDF Download Accordion
* Endorsed By Leading Architects & Designer Reviews Section
* Instagram Social Shoppable Grid
* Sticky Add-to-Cart Bar on PDP
* Advanced Faceted Search Filters with Grid View Controls (2/3/4-col & List)
* High Performance Lighthouse Score (>90 Desktop)
* Clean Liquid Standard & 100% Theme Check Validated
